# Email Marketing Best Practices for CBD Businesses

Email marketing can help CBD and hemp businesses maintain communication with people who have chosen to receive updates from them. It provides a direct channel for sharing educational resources, company announcements, and relevant business information.

However, CBD-related email campaigns require careful planning because product regulations and marketing platform policies can vary by location and provider.

Here are some important practices to consider.

## 1. Build a Permission-Based Email List

A reliable email list should consist of people who have clearly agreed to receive communications.

Businesses should use transparent subscription forms and explain what subscribers can expect.

Avoid purchasing email lists or adding people without their permission.

## 2. Understand Your Audience

Different subscribers may have different interests and information needs.

Some may prefer educational articles, while others may want company updates or general industry information.

Audience research can help businesses create more relevant email content.

## 3. Share Informative Content

Email newsletters should provide useful information rather than focusing entirely on promotions.

Possible content ideas include:

- CBD industry news and updates
- Educational articles about hemp
- Product labeling information
- Third-party testing explanations
- Company announcements
- General business resources

Keep information accurate and avoid unsupported medical or health claims.

## 4. Write Clear Subject Lines

A subject line should explain what an email contains without misleading the recipient.

Use simple language, avoid exaggerated promises, and make sure the message delivers what the subject line communicates.

## 5. Organize Your Email Campaigns

A consistent email schedule can help businesses communicate more effectively.

Create a content calendar that identifies the purpose, audience, topic, and publishing date of each message.

Avoid sending excessive emails that may reduce subscriber interest.

## 6. Measure Email Performance

Campaign analytics can help businesses understand how subscribers interact with their messages.

Useful indicators include:

- Delivery rate
- Open rate, where reliably measurable
- Click-through rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Bounce rate
- Website visits from email campaigns

Use these insights to improve future communication.

## 7. Follow Email Marketing Regulations

CBD businesses should review applicable email marketing laws, privacy requirements, and the policies of their email service providers.

Include accurate sender information, provide a working unsubscribe option, and respect subscriber preferences.

Also check whether the provider permits CBD-related business communications.

## Conclusion

Email marketing can support customer communication when it is based on permission, relevant information, and responsible practices.

CBD and hemp businesses should focus on useful content, clear messaging, subscriber privacy, and compliance with applicable requirements.

A thoughtful email strategy can help maintain meaningful communication and support a consistent digital presence.
